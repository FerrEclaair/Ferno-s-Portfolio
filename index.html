      03 About

      Behind the data

      I'm Ferno, an Information Systems student at President University on the Data Science track. I enjoy turning raw data into insights people can act on, building analytical tools, and figuring out how technology solves practical problems.

      Most of my projects start with a practical question: what is happening, why is it happening, and what should we do next? I enjoy working across the full data workflow, from database design and SQL to machine learning, dashboards, APIs, and orchestration.

      The parts I care about are data analytics, machine learning, data visualization, database systems, data engineering, and applied AI. I like the whole path, not just the model. Schema design, query speed, and the labels on an axis decide whether an analysis gets used or ignored.

      Data is only useful when someone can act on it.
